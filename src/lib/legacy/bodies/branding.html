Frequently Asked Questions

01. What does OTT’s branding service include?

Our branding service covers everything from developing a brand strategy and visual identity (like logo and color palette) to style guides to maintain consistency across all communication channels.

02. How long does it take to develop a complete brand identity?

The branding process usually takes 4 to 8 weeks, depending on project complexity and client collaboration. We ensure time is taken to fully understand your business and create a brand that resonates with your audience.

03. Why should I invest in branding rather than just advertising?

Branding establishes the foundation of your business and defines how customers perceive you in the long run. While advertising attracts immediate customers, branding builds lasting relationships and loyalty, encouraging repeat business.

04. Will my brand look the same across all channels?

Yes, one of our primary goals is to ensure consistency. We create style guides and visual resources that make it easy to maintain a cohesive look and tone on social media, websites, and other touchpoints.

05.  What types of businesses can benefit from OTT’s branding?

Our branding services benefit any business looking to stand out and deeply connect with its audience, regardless of size or industry. From startups to established companies, everyone can benefit from a strong, professional brand.
